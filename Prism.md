# The Prism Pitch: ReGenAI

**Samsung Prism 2026 - Bengaluru**

## Core Talking Points

### The Problem
Engineering students face "**Feasibility Blindness**" and an "**Implementation Void**". When interacting with state-of-the-art research (e.g., ArXiv), the theoretical density makes it incredibly difficult to identify which projects are practically buildable within a reasonable timeframe, leading to abandoned projects and wasted hours on 'bit-rotted' repositories.

### The Solution
**ReGenAI**—an autonomous agent powered by the OpenClaw framework and Antigravity automation. It acts as an intelligent Research-to-Implementation bridge that:
1. Audits GitHub repositories for bit-rot and generates a Build Feasibility Score (0-100).
2. Summarizes dense theoretical papers into actionable, API-driven implementation guides.
3. Plans the work by converting the research into 4-week execution roadmaps (Gantt-style).

### The Impact
ReGenAI drastically reduces the "**time-to-first-run**" for modern engineers. By automatically filtering out [HIGH COMPLEXITY] and outdated repositories, it ensures students and professionals spend more time *building* and less time *troubleshooting* unbuildable academic code.
